About Me

Lindo Lance, a 23-year-old resident of San Juan City, is embarking on his academic journey as a first-year college student at FEU Institute of Technology, where he is pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology with a specialization in Cybersecurity. Known for his determination and resilience, Lindo eagerly embraces new learning opportunities and faces challenges head-on. His curiosity and enthusiasm for learning drive him to explore and tackle new projects, continually expanding his knowledge and skills. Growing up in a small village has significantly shaped Lindo's creative and recreational interests. He has developed a deep appreciation for various activities, including basketball, billiards, and motorcycle riding. These hobbies not only provide him with enjoyment and relaxation but also reflect his adventurous spirit and willingness to try new things. Lindo's background and experiences have instilled in him a well-rounded personality, balancing his technical pursuits with a passion for diverse and enriching activities.
